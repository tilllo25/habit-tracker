Habit Tracker

Mindful living, one day at a time

Small steps. Every day.

A calm, simple habit tracker for anyone who wants to live more consciously. Drink more water, move every day or make time to breathe: build the routines that make you feel good, without pressure or clutter.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add your habits

    Name what you want to build, with an optional note on why or how.

  2. 2

    Tick them off daily

    Check a habit when it's done and see your progress for today at a glance.

  3. 3

    Watch your week grow

    The 7-day view shows your rhythm, so small wins turn into lasting routines.

Private by design: your habits are stored only in this browser. No account, no tracking.
